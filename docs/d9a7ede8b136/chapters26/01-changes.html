2  What changed since the thesis

Which differences between the 2023 chapter and the present analyses change the findings, and by how much? Table 2.1 lists the twelve largest, ordered by consequence for the conclusions. The sections after it explain the problems with the 2023 methods and how each was fixed, then list the new analyses and the new direction.

The full 41-row comparison with the deposited chapter is in Appendix E.

2.1 Problems with the 2023 methods and the fixes

What was wrong with the original analysis, and what replaced it? Seven problems matter.

The pyroxene diffusivity bracket. The 2023 fits bracketed the pyroxene diffusivity with laws at T ± 20 K. The upper cpx value in that bracket was extrapolated from Ferriss et al. (2016) to the tetrahedral Al of the WHT1 cpx; they publish laws for Kunlun diopside but no equation for the Al dependence. The cpx interior profiles were fit with the opx bracket, and the opx interior profile of GCB duplicated the WHT1 exterior profile. These diffusivities are faster than the fits now need, which made the pyroxene rates (0.69 and 0.68 MPa/s) disagree with olivine. Leaving D free gives opx −10.98 and cpx −11.50 in log10 m²/s at 1200 °C, matching slow natural-pyroxene values, and a joint χ² of 20.6 for 20 points.

Isothermal 1200 °C. The thesis modeled the whole ascent at 1200 °C. Along the P–T path the melt is at 1110–1180 °C where most H2O leaves, so the isothermal diffusivity is too high. The path rate equals an isothermal fit at about 1150 °C, the mean entrapment temperature of the SR inclusions.

The degassing path start. The 2023 path began from the GCB lava and implied no water loss until 150 MPa. The MiMiC start saturates at 682 MPa and loses half of its H2O only below about 55 MPa (CO2/Nb start: 922 and 63 MPa). It still holds 90 % of its H2O at 248 MPa (CO2/Nb start: 340 MPa). The 2023 path is reproduced exactly with its own inputs, so the difference is the start, not the code.

SIMS combined uncertainty. The deposited combined uncertainty (about 0.58× the 1σ) understated the error on each H2O value. A total 1σ combining calibration, counting and background replaces it, with detection limits and quantification limits per session. One cpx value (T1-216) was re-reduced on the opx line (129.70 against 131.26 ppm).

The olivine partition coefficient prior. The MATLAB prior was N(9, 0.5) × 10⁻⁴, with an SD 6× narrower than the ±0.0003 in the source paper. The mean is unchanged, and the wider range lets K move the rate as it should (K = 6 raises it 2.29×; K = 12 lowers it 0.58×).

GC2_ol4 volatiles. The 2023 inclusion table gave GC2_ol4 the volatiles of GC2_ol3 (H2O 2.39, CO2 3,136 ppm before correction). GC2_ol4 has no measured volatiles and is now excluded from the inclusion tables.

The Fig. 11 caption. The caption printed the olivine/melt partition coefficient as 0.009 ± 0.003, ten times the 0.0009 ± 0.0003 the scripts and text use.

2.2 New analyses

Which analyses did the thesis not have? The list below names them; each is developed in the chapter shown.

  • A temperature path from the mantle start to the surface, with the rate warped onto it (Chapter 8, Appendix A).
  • A joint fit of olivine, opx and cpx with free pyroxene diffusivity, and a stall test (Chapter 8).
  • Fe–Mg residence times with FORSTER for Ol-2 and the xenocrysts (Chapter 9).
  • Coupled and nested grain-boundary fits, with buffering of exterior grains (Chapter 8).
  • Original and modified MiMiC runs and the CO2/Nb estimate of undegassed CO2 (Chapter 6, Appendix B).
  • Alternative olivine laws, edge treatments and solubility models (Appendix A, Appendix C).
  • A like-for-like comparison with published rates on each study’s own model temperature (Chapter 8).
  • Detection limits and the total uncertainty for every H2O value (Appendix D).

2.3 New direction

Where does the work go from here? The working hypothesis is that the host magma resets the NAMs, so the xenoliths record the ascent and not the mantle. The interior opx cores equal the MiMiC melt within about 2 %, and the data constrain how fast the host’s falling water activity is transmitted inward. Three consequences follow. Temperature along the path is part of the rate, not a footnote. The fits are joint, so one ascent must explain olivine, opx and cpx at once. The next tests are the partitioning of cpx, the CO2/Nb scenario and the starting level of the opx (Chapter 11).
