Appendix E — Decisions, crosswalk and reproducibility

This appendix is the record behind the report: the decisions taken and their evidence, the 41 claims of the deposited Chapter 3 against their 2026 status, the scripts that regenerate every number, and the dated working logs the chapters were written from. Dates and working-document names appear only here.

E.1 Decisions

Table E.1 lists the choices behind the central rate and the report, sorted by date. Letter-number codes (A1, B4, D5) are the question numbers of the supplement question list; “plan answer” refers to the answers recorded for the summary workbook; “review section 9” is the set of decisions on the rebuilt report.

The newest decisions change the framing more than the numbers. The headline is now the joint fit of olivine, opx and cpx on the P–T path (0.100 MPa/s, 0.050–0.200 over 46–120 °C/GPa, MiMiC start) rather than the olivine-only 0.093 MPa/s; continuous ascent is the best history at the central and steepest slopes (a 5-minute stall fits better only at 46 °C/GPa); the published MiMiC melt replaced the CO2/Nb start as the primary start on 2026-10-30; and the report leads with what changed since the 2023 chapter.

E.2 Crosswalk to the deposited Chapter 3

The deposited Chapter 3 (Testing the Fidelity of Peridotite Xenoliths as Records of Water in the Lithospheric Mantle) is the 2023 baseline. Table E.2 lists its substantive claims, results and interpretations in the order of the chapter and gives each a status against the 2026 analyses:

  • Confirmed: the 2026 work supports the point.
  • Revised: the point stands in outline but the number or the method changed.
  • Superseded: the 2026 work replaces it.
  • Extended: the point stands and was tested further.
  • Not revisited: the 2026 sources do not address it.

Statuses rest on the working logs in Section E.4. The text-versus-data conflicts that predate this work (the olivine sentences read about 1.3 times the SIMS dataset values) are in Section D.6. No value of the SIMS dataset was changed.

New in 2026 (not in Chapter 3).

  • Temperature along the magma P–T path. A straight path from 1330 ± 15 °C at 2.26 ± 0.10 GPa; the families (Towbin and Plank 2026, a fit through 12 SR inclusions, and the SR-wide envelope) give 1176 to 1107 °C at the surface and slow every rate by a common factor (Chapter 3, Chapter 8).
  • Localities and magma pulses. Pit and roadside samples are probably different pulses; roadside inclusions are hotter at the same pressure (1148–1198 against 1139–1166 °C).
  • Fe–Mg residence times and Fo populations. FORSTER times for Ol-2 (18 d) and the xenocrysts, rims in equilibrium with the lava, and the lower-MgO final melt that no measured inclusion samples (Section A.10, Section D.8).
  • MiMiC versions. Original (reproduced exactly), 2026 baseline and modified (Duan & Zhang bubble CO2) runs; the version moves the ascent rates by 2 % or less and the P–T slope by 0.2 °C/GPa (Appendix B).
  • Solubility temperature. Evaluating MagmaSat along isotherms of 1050–1300 °C changes the rate by 3 % or less (Section C.4).
  • Literature model temperatures. Each published rate placed on its own model temperature, so the SR rate is compared like for like (Chapter 8).
  • Interior-core anchoring of the start. Intact interior WHT1 opx cores fix the starting melt H2O at 3.48 wt%, within 2 % of the MiMiC start (3.549) and 3 % of SR-1; two grains flatten near 300–320 ppm, which is treated as a K or starting-H2O uncertainty (Chapter 9).
  • Joint fit with free pyroxene D, on the P–T path. One continuous ascent fits olivine, opx and cpx at 0.100 MPa/s; no stall is needed at the central slope (Chapter 8, Section A.9).
  • Uncertainty and detection limits. Total 1σ, L_C, L_D and LoQ for every analysis; fixed fit σ kept (Appendix D).
  • Barth edge effects on olivine. Two-population models fit Ol-2 only if exchange is fast, and widening the final-pressure prior moves the isothermal rate only within 0.155–0.164 MPa/s on the CO2/Nb start (Section A.8).
  • Alternative olivine laws and solubility models. Peterson D_OH and power law, Barth laws, and Iacono-Marziano against MagmaSat (Appendix A, Appendix C).
  • Grain-boundary fits and mechanisms. Coupled and nested grain-boundary fits on the joint-fit scenarios, buffering of the exterior grains, and the melt, grain-boundary and hybrid pathways (Chapter 8).
  • Cpx partitioning misfit and CO2/Nb. The cpx cores hold 1.2–2.6 times the water the recalibrated experiments predict; the start CO2 is a CO2/Nb estimate (Chapter 9, Section B.7).

E.3 Reproducibility: scripts, outputs, commands

One table per topic lists the script, what it does and where its output goes. All paths are relative to the repository root (Ch3_Xenolith_SIMS_Clean, private). Each script’s module docstring gives its command; the patterns are below. Scripts without a documented purpose, shared helpers and FTIR scripts (scripts/ftir/, out of scope) are not listed. The dated working documents that describe these runs are in Section E.4.

The external code the scripts call is in Table E.3.

Environments.

  • NAM environment (all decompression fits). The posterior code lives in the H2O_Diffusion_NAM repository (public). Fit scripts in scripts/decompression/ run as PYTHONPATH=~/Projects/H2O_Diffusion_NAM uv run --project ~/Projects/H2O_Diffusion_NAM --with pyarrow --with scipy python scripts/decompression/<script>.py. Plot-only scripts need only uv run --with pandas --with matplotlib.
  • MagmaSat degassing paths (container). MagmaSat runs inside the ENKI ThermoEngine container (VESIcal 1.2.10). scripts/degassing_paths/run_magmasat.sh [N] starts N parallel containers (through colima and docker), skips cached paths, and writes JSON files to data/degassing_paths_2026/magmasat/cache/. The fast models (Iacono-Marziano, Dixon) run on the host with --with VESIcal==1.2.12.
  • MiMiC. The vendored code in scripts/mimic/vendor/ (Rasmussen et al. 2020) is unmodified (PROVENANCE.md records the upstream commit). scripts/mimic/run_mimic.py runs it with uv run --no-project --python 3.13 --with numpy==2.2.6 --with scipy==1.16.3.
  • Fe–Mg diffusion. FORSTER, from the Olivine_Diffusion_FeMg repository: uv run --project ~/Projects/Olivine_Diffusion_FeMg forster fit ....
  • Everything else uses uv run --with <packages> or the project .venv as in each script’s docstring.

Rebuild order for the numbers in the report.

  1. Data: .venv/bin/python scripts/build_compiled_workbook.py, then scripts/recalc.py (needs LibreOffice), scripts/verify_compiled.py and scripts/export_csv.py.
  2. Supplement and summary workbook: .venv/bin/python scripts/build_supplement.py 2026, then scripts/supplement/summary_workbook.py (15 sheets, one CSV per sheet in data/supplement/summary_csv/) and scripts/supplement/check_summary.py (exits 1 on any failure).
  3. Starting melts and degassing paths: scripts/original_volatiles/, scripts/degassing_paths/ and scripts/mimic/.
  4. Fits: fit_wet_primary.py, fit_pt_path.py, joint_d_fit.py (isothermal, then --slope 46, 82.8 and 120 on the P–T path), then the exchange scripts with --path wet --rate 0.17783 (isothermal) and --path wet_pt --slope S --rate R for each joint-fit scenario.
  5. The book: cd report && python3 tools/check_links.py, then quarto render and python3 tools/build_pdf.py.

E.4 Working logs

The chapters of this report are the synthesis. The documents in Table E.12 are the dated working logs they were written from: each records what was asked, what was run and what came out on a given day, and some were superseded by later ones. Where a log and a chapter disagree, the chapter and the later-dated log win. The logs are in the private repository and are linked by relative path. FTIR documents are left out (out of scope for this report). The scripts behind the logs are in Section E.3.
